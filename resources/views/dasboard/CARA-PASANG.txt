Dashboard Hotel (Laravel + Bootstrap 5)

Salin ke proyek Laravel:
  app/Http/Controllers/DashboardController.php
  resources/views/dashboard.blade.php
  resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php

Tambahkan di routes/web.php:
  use App\Http\Controllers\DashboardController;
  Route::get('/', [DashboardController::class, 'index'])->name('dashboard');

Catatan: navbar dan kartu memakai route tipe-kamar.index, kamar.index, tamu.index, reservasi.index.
Buat dulu route-nya, atau hapus sementara menu/tautan yang belum ada.
